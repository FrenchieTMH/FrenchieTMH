### Hi, I'm Frenchie

I'm building **Ashquill**, a fan-made Elder Scrolls companion for Discord. It brings random lore and NPC quotes
from the Unofficial Elder Scrolls Pages (UESP) to your server, runs trivia and guess-the-quote games,
and keeps a leaderboard so everyone can settle who really knows Tamriel best.
